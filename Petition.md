
# The Basics 
The most important thing we have to remember when drafting this petition is that data belongs to the user

- Service providers have no right over the data produced by the users. 
- Service providers cant sell or share users data without their permission 
- Service providers must be clear with who and to what extend each users data is shared to third parties
- Service providers cannot auto enroll users into programs outside what the user is signing up for
tickboxes such as subscribe to newsletters cannot be auto checked. The user must consent themselves
- Users has the right to delete ALL their data
- Users also have the right to demand a copy of all of their data in its raw format.
- Users also have the right to receive their data in a readable format as well.  
no more CD, might as well give a floppy disk when requesting data
- Users have the right to easily opt out of features that use their data for ads/profiling. 
- No more auto enrolling to unnecessary services.

# issues
## our laws are outdated and does not meet the global standards   
Developed nations such as the EU recognized the issues relating to data sovereignty and has established a GDPR standard which Maldives has yet to adopt or follow
## Users don't have any control over their data when it comes to deletion or access  
### Issues relating to Data Deletion 
The same way copyright works for artworks it applies the same for any data produced by a user. Without a proper law a delete button does not mean anything. 
In most cases when a user requests for deletion that data is simply hidden from all users. The data still persists on the servers. This is called soft deletion. With laws and regulations we can ensure that the delete button does what we expect it to do. Delete the data 
### Issues relating to Data Access
Users must be able to get access to their data. Companies cannot hold the users data hostage and must provide an easy solution to get a copy of the data
## Increase in scams and identity theft due to lack of a data protection law  
The reason for the increase in scams and identity theft is because companies are not afraid of data leaking. Data is leaking from multiple companies and this data is compiled to build profiles on people which then gets sold and used to run scams or other identity theft related crimes. 

## Cyber crime law is not enough we also need a data protection law to ensure those handling our data is handling it in a secure manner  
The thing with cyber crime law is that it makes it illegal to hack into servers. But it does not make it illegal for servers to be unprotected. It does not make it illegal for companies to not report a hack. Companies are not required to disclose the extent of the hack to their customers. Companies can deny any mistake on their end.

## With the advancement of AI the lack of a law for data protection will make the issue even worse  
Ai needs a lot of data for its training. The question is do we want AI to use our data for its training? What data of ours do we consent for the AI to use for training? Do we consent to our faces being used? Just because we give camera permissions is that enough consent that our pictures be used for ai training?
Data protection must include a clause that says consent for AI training must be verbose 
It cant be “to use our app please agree to TOS”
It must be “to use this feature you will be agreeing to let use use your chats and pictures to train our ai”
See how suddenly it's a lot more clear. 
TOS cannot be used to hide serious data abuse 
If this is not included in the data protection it must be an AI regulation bill of its own 
## We cant go forward with a maldives 2.0 without a data protection law in place  
Maldives 2.0 cannot and will not happen without the following laws and regulators 
- Data protection 
- Ai regulation 
- Cyber safety act
- Data protection authority
